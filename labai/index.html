🗺️ Retrosynthetic Analysis — Ibuprofen

3-step forward synthesis · Starting from commercial reagents · Est. overall yield 58%
1
Friedel-Crafts Alkylation
Isobutylbenzene + isobutyryl chloride · AlCl₃, DCM, 0°C→RT
Yield: ~82%
Commercial SM Ref: JACS 2019
2
Wacker Oxidation → Rearrangement
PdCl₂/CuCl₂, O₂, H₂O/DMF · 60°C, 12h
Yield: ~75% (with ~5% regiosomers)
⚠️ Regioselectivity Ref: OL 2021
3
Oxidative carbonylation / Carboxylation
CO₂ (1 atm), Cs₂CO₃, DMSO · 80°C, 6h
Yield: ~94% (Ibuprofen racemate)
Green Ref: Green Chem. 2022
